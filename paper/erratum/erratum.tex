The listed references contained incorrect bibliographic metadata; the corrections do not affect any result or claim.

\section*{Reference [2]}
\noindent\textbf{As printed:} Zachary Ankner, Mansheej Paul Abraham, Yu Bai Chen, Kenji Cho, Jonas Geiping, Tom Goldstein, RJ Gupta, Mark McKenzie, Marco Noci, Jonathan Pilault, et al. Hydra: Sequentially-dependent draft heads for medusa decoding. arXiv preprint arXiv:2402.05109, 2024.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{What was wrong:} The author list was incorrect. Only Zachary Ankner is an author of this paper; the other named authors do not appear in the arXiv record, and the paper has six authors in total, so ``et al.'' was also wrong. The title, identifier and year were correct.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Corrected reference:} Zachary Ankner, Rishab Parthasarathy, Aniruddha Nrusimha, Christopher Rinard, Jonathan Ragan-Kelley, and William Brandon. Hydra: Sequentially-dependent draft heads for medusa decoding. arXiv preprint arXiv:2402.05109, 2024.
